Module 04 — Mean Reversion

The second strategy archetype. When the price wanders but is pulled back, bet on the pull.

← curriculum home

What you'll know after this module: What mean reversion is, mathematically (AR(1), OU). How to estimate the parameters from data. How to compute a z-score and a half-life. How to backtest a z-score strategy on past data. Why mean reversion fails when the spread is wide. The two failure modes (jumps that are too big; trends that don't revert).

Why this module comes third

Module 03 taught market-making — capturing the spread, controlling inventory. This module teaches the second archetype: bet the price returns to a center. The two strategies are complementary: market-making earns the spread, mean reversion earns the directional move. Most P3/P4 products (STARFRUIT, ASH, FRUIT, ORCHIDS) are mean-reverting, and the team that didn't get mean reversion right didn't survive past Round 3 in P4.

Where this shows up in Prosperity

  • STARFRUIT (P3, P4) — the canonical mean-reverting asset.
  • ASH, FRUIT (P3, P4) — mean-reverting, also traded as options in Round 5.
  • ORCHIDS — partly mean-reverting around a fundamental value.
  • Pairs of correlated products — cointegration (module 05) is the multivariate version of this.
