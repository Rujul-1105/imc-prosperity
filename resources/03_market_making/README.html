Module 03 — Market Making

The simplest strategy archetype. Quote both sides, capture the spread, manage inventory.

← curriculum home

What you'll know after this module: The Avellaneda–Stoikov formula and what each parameter does. Timo's Wall-Mid shortcut and why it often beats AS. How to estimate the parameters from data. How to combine both with inventory caps. How to backtest a market-making strategy on a past round and decompose the PnL.

Why this module comes second

Module 02 taught you the order book. This module teaches you the simplest strategy you can run on it: market making. The whole point of an order book is to provide a place for market-makers to post quotes. Once you can read a book and post quotes, you have a strategy that works on every Prosperity round that has any tradeable product.

Where this shows up in Prosperity

  • AMETHYSTS (FFV) — the canonical market-making round. AS or Wall-Mid both work. The team should master this archetype first.
  • RAINFOREST_RESIN (P3/P4 R1) — Timo's Wall-Mid won P3 R1 on this.
  • SQUID_INK (P3 R1) — GBM-style. Tighter spreads required; higher inventory churn.
  • STARFRUIT, ASH, FRUIT — market-making is the secondary play; primary is mean-reversion. But you still post quotes when prices are stable.
  • Every round — even basket and options rounds reward a base market-making strategy on the underlying.

Honest assessment

Market-making is not the highest-PnL strategy in most rounds. The higher-PnL plays are basket arb, options pricing, cross-exchange arb. But market-making is the most consistent — every round has tradeable product, and a working market-maker will not lose money in any of them. It is the strategy you fall back on when nothing else works. Master it first.

Furthermore, the underlying insights — inventory risk, adverse selection, parameter estimation — are the foundation for every other strategy. Learn them here.
