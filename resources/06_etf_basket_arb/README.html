Module 06 — ETF / Basket Arbitrage

The structural edge the team missed in P4. If you can identify a basket and its constituents, the trade is mechanical.

← curriculum home

What you'll know after this module: What a basket (or synthetic ETF) is. How to derive basket weights from the round spec or from data. How to compute NAV (net asset value) every tick. The structural arb trade: when market price diverges from NAV, buy the cheap side and sell the rich side. How to size and unwind the position. The PEBBLE-style "inner rebalancing" trick that gives zero-risk PnL.

Why this module comes early (week 7)

The team's P4 attempt dropped out at Round 3. The post-mortem suggests they didn't solve basket arb cleanly. Basket rounds are a top-3 differentiator in P3 and P4 — teams that nailed it climbed past teams that didn't. This module is the curriculum's corrective.

Files in this module

explain.html — what a basket is, why the trade is mechanical. source.html — BIS ETF paper, Timo's P3 R3 GIFT_BASKET code, supplementary reading. extract.html — NAV formula, weights solver, trade signal, inner rebalancing. exercises.html — 5 hands-on exercises on P3/P4 data. code/solve_weights.py — solve for basket weights via OLS. code/nav.py — compute NAV and the basket-vs-NAV spread. code/basket_arb.py — full basket arb strategy + backtester. code/inner_rebalance.py — PEBBLE-style zero-risk inner rebalancing.

Where this shows up in Prosperity

  • GIFT_BASKET (P3 R3, P4 R?) = 4×CHOCOLATE + 6×STRAWBERRIES + 1×ROSES. Canonical basket.
  • PEBBLE (P4) = some weighted sum of 5 assets = 50,000. Inner rebalancing gives pure PnL.
  • PICNIC_BASKET (P3 R2) = similar pattern.
  • VOLCANIC_ROCK / VOUCHER_* (P4) = voucher pairs, a different but related structure.
