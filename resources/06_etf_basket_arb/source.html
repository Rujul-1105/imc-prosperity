Module 06 — Sources

The paper, the practice, and the supplementary reading.

← module README

Main source #1 — the paper

BIS Working Paper, "Anatomy of ETF Arbitrage." Available at bis.org → search "ETF arbitrage."

The cleanest modern treatment of how ETF arbitrage actually works. Covers authorized participants, creation/redemption, the spread dynamics, and the failure modes. Read the whole paper; it's short.

Main source #2 — the practice

Timo Diehm, imc-prosperity-3, 2nd place in P3. round_3_strategy.py.

The basket-arb code Timo used on GIFT_BASKET. Simple, mechanical. Read it in full; the structure is < 50 lines.

Supplement 1 — Box 1 of the BIS paper

The "Box 1" in the BIS paper walks through a numerical example of ETF arbitrage. Read this box even if you skim the rest — it makes the abstract concrete.

Supplement 2 — pe049395/IMC-Prosperity-2024

The Korean team in P4 that got 2nd in Round 2. They used cross-sectional OLS for basket weights. Useful as a second reference.

What this module does NOT cover

  • Creation/redemption mechanics — not applicable in Prosperity (no AP).
  • Tax implications — none in Prosperity.
  • Options on baskets — not in the standard round lineup.
