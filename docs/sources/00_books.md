# Books — Canonical Sources

Every book the curriculum draws from. Listed in order of importance for the curriculum.

---

## Larry Harris — Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners (2003)

- **Why**: The single best market microstructure book. Used in most quant programs.
- **Used in**: Module 02 (Microstructure), Module 17 (Advanced microstructure).
- **Read**: Ch 1-7, 12-14. Skim Ch 8-11, 15-22.
- **Get it**:
  - OUP print: ~$80.
  - USC faculty page: chapters posted as free PDFs (Google "Harris Trading and Exchanges market microstructure book chapters PDF" if the link moves).
  - Library.

## John Hull — Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives (10th ed, 2017)

- **Why**: The standard options pricing textbook. Clear, comprehensive.
- **Used in**: Module 07 (Options & Greeks).
- **Read**: Ch 12-17. Ch 15 (Black-Scholes), Ch 17 (Greek letters) are the densest.
- **Get it**: Print ~$80. Library. Older editions are 90% as good and cheaper.

## Ernie Chan — Algorithmic Trading (2013)

- **Why**: Best practical guide to mean reversion and pairs trading.
- **Used in**: Module 04 (Mean reversion), Module 05 (Cointegration).
- **Read**: Ch 4 (mean reversion), Ch 5 (cointegration), Ch 6-8 (pairs trading).
- **Get it**: Print ~$50. Library.

## Marcos López de Prado — Advances in Financial Machine Learning (2018)

- **Why**: The standard reference for ML in finance. Specifically about how to *not* overfit.
- **Used in**: Module 12 (ML signals).
- **Read**: Ch 1-7.
- **Get it**: Print ~$80. Library.

## Allen Downey — Think Bayes (2013)

- **Why**: Free online. Bayesian statistics with code. Beginner-friendly.
- **Used in**: Module 11 (Bayesian signals).
- **Read**: Ch 1-6.
- **Get it**: Free at https://www.greenteapress.com/wp/think-bayes/

## Ralph Vince — The Mathematics of Money Management (1992)

- **Why**: The standard reference on Kelly criterion and position sizing for trading.
- **Used in**: Module 14 (Risk & sizing).
- **Read**: Ch 1-2.
- **Get it**: Print ~$60. Library.

## Avellaneda & Stoikov (paper, not a book) — see sources/01_papers.md

## Irene Aldridge — High-Frequency Trading (2013)

- **Why**: Covers HFT, market microstructure, market-making at the math level. Good supplement to Harris.
- **Used in**: Modules 02, 03, 17.
- **Read**: Ch 1-5.
- **Get it**: Print ~$80. Library.

## Cartea, Jaimungal, Penã — Algorithmic Trading (2015)

- **Why**: Mathematical treatment of algorithmic trading. Stochastic control, market-making variants.
- **Used in**: Module 18 (Stochastic control, advanced).
- **Read**: Ch 1-2.
- **Get it**: Print ~$70. Library.

## Leo Boukamp — Heard on the Street (recent ed)

- **Why**: Quantitative interview puzzles. Builds the kind of thinking Prosperity tests.
- **Used in**: Module 19 (Manual rounds) and general interview prep.
- **Read**: Cover to cover over time.
- **Get it**: Print ~$30.

## Free alternatives

If the team can't buy books:

- **Harris chapters** are free on his USC page.
- **Downey's Think Bayes** is free.
- **Hull's Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives** has older editions free on the author's university page (Google "Hull options futures derivatives 7th edition PDF").
- **Chan's Algorithmic Trading** has key chapters excerpted online.
- **López de Prado's AFML** has a companion GitHub repo `BlackArbsCEO/Adv_Fin_ML_Exercises` with exercises.
